# BRAND.md

## Purpose

This document captures INTEGRALA's brand positioning, messaging, and boundaries for collaborators and future agents working on the website, copy, or communications.

It separates **established decisions** (approved by the current brief) from **assumptions that still need human approval**.

## Status

- **Company stage:** Early-stage company.
- **Commercial proof:** No established commercial case studies, customer logos, testimonials, or published measurable results yet.
- **Primary goal right now:** Start conversations with potential customers.

## Positioning (established)

INTEGRALA is an **engineering company** that helps real-world businesses improve and evolve through technology.

**Core message:** *Hacemos que los negocios funcionen mejor con tecnología.*

We are **not** an AI company and **not** a generic software factory. We are an engineering partner that understands business problems and builds solutions through:

- **System integration:** connecting systems so information flows and tools work together.
- **Process automation:** reducing manual work and simplifying how operations run.
- **Custom software development:** building solutions when off-the-shelf tools are not enough.
- **Modernization and evolution of existing systems:** improving what already exists to support new needs and growth.

Technology is a means, not an end. **AI is just one tool** we use when it genuinely adds value.

Our goal is to improve how businesses operate, how people work, and how information flows.

## Target industries (initial focus, established)

These are examples of businesses and problems we can help solve, **not claims of previous projects or customers**.

- **Supermarkets and retail:** inventory, replenishment, pricing, promotions, branch coordination, POS/ERP integration, logistics.
- **Restaurants and restaurant groups:** orders across channels, delivery integration, inventory, purchasing, kitchen operations, multi-branch management.
- **Ecommerce and online businesses:** order management, inventory synchronization, marketplaces, payments, logistics, automation, scalability.

## Audience and tone (established)

- **Primary audience:** Business owners and operations managers (not only technical audiences).
- **Language/tone:** Natural Argentine Spanish (**voseo**), professional, direct, approachable.
- **Style:** Concrete and plain. Explain what we improve for businesses rather than listing technologies.
- **Engineering expertise:** Preserve engineering expertise as a central differentiator, expressed through judgment, understanding, and reliable solutions—not jargon.

## Messaging principles (established)

- Lead with the business problem and the operational improvement.
- Show that we first understand how the business works before proposing solutions.
- Make it easy for people to approach us even if they don't know exactly what technical solution they need.
- Keep technology, including AI, in a supporting role unless it is the clearest answer to the problem.
- Avoid repetition and unnecessary sections.

## Preferred messaging examples

### Good

- "Hacemos que los negocios funcionen mejor con tecnología."
- "Entendemos cómo funciona tu negocio y desarrollamos tecnología para hacerlo evolucionar."
- "Muchas veces el problema no es la falta de software, sino cómo se conectan las herramientas existentes, cómo circula la información o cómo se ejecutan determinados procesos."
- "Conectamos sistemas para que compartan información y trabajen juntos."
- "Contanos qué está pasando en tu negocio. No hace falta que tengas definida la solución."
- "Si usás varias herramientas que no se hablan entre sí, podemos ayudarte a ordenar ese flujo."

### Bad

- "Somos una empresa líder en transformación digital impulsada por IA." (generic, AI-overemphasized, exaggerated)
- "Revolucionamos tu negocio con soluciones disruptivas de última generación." (cliché, vague, inflated)
- "Implementamos stacks cloud-native, microservicios y modelos LLM de vanguardia." (technology-first, not audience-appropriate)
- "Ya ayudamos a cientos de empresas a aumentar sus ventas un 300%." (invented customers/results)
- "Trabajamos con las principales cadenas de supermercados y restaurantes del país." (invented customers/experience claims)

## Claims to avoid (established)

- Invented customers, client logos, testimonials, case studies, partnerships, or project histories.
- Measurable results, ROI figures, percentages, time savings, or growth claims without verified evidence.
- Exaggerated promises, guarantees, or "leader/best/disruptive" positioning.
- Overemphasizing AI or technology trends as the main value proposition.
- Generic marketing clichés detached from concrete business problems.
- Implying deep industry specialization based on past work we do not have; industry examples are target problems/opportunities, not credentials.

## Role of AI (established)

- AI is **one possible tool**, not our identity.
- Mention AI only when relevant to a concrete problem or capability, and never as the headline promise.
- Prefer explaining the operational improvement; if AI is involved, frame it as part of a broader engineering solution.
